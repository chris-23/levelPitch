# Testing LevelPitch

Thanks for trying LevelPitch! The app measures how your motorhome or caravan
is tilted, using your phone lying inside it, and tells you which wedge step
to put under which wheel (for caravans also how far to wind the jockey
wheel). This is an early test version, and your feedback decides what gets
fixed next.

**Safety first:** handbrake on and wheels chocked before you leave the
vehicle. Never lift a caravan with its corner steadies.

The app follows your phone's language (English or German); ⋮ → *Settings*
switches it. German guide: [TESTING.de.md](TESTING.de.md).

## 1. Set up (once, 5 minutes)

1. **Vehicle:** menu ⋮ → *Vehicles & wedges* → *Add vehicle*.
   - Motorhome: pick your base vehicle (Fiat Ducato, Sprinter, …) and
     wheelbase. The dimensions fill in by themselves. If you know your
     exact wheelbase and track, enter those.
   - Caravan: pick the size closest to yours. If you like, measure the
     track (tyre centre to tyre centre) and the distance from the coupling
     ball to the wheel hub, and enter them.
2. **Wedges:** *Add wedge set*, then pick your model or enter the step
   heights. If you have a jack, air bags or curved levellers, choose
   *Lifts to any height* and enter the maximum lift.
3. **Measuring spot:** choose a flat spot inside that is part of the
   vehicle, e.g. the floor next to a cupboard. Lay the phone flat, screen
   up, **pushed against something straight** (a cupboard edge, a strip
   of tape). Always use the same spot, the same way round. Tell the app
   which way the top edge of the phone points.

## 2. Calibrate (once per vehicle)

The app needs to know what "level" means for your measuring spot.

1. Park somewhere as level as you can get. Check with a spirit level on
   the floor, both along and across the vehicle, and use wedges until the
   bubble is centred.
2. Menu ⋮ → *Calibration* → phone on your spot → *Set zero*.

If you ever use the phone the other way round, set a zero for that
direction too.

## 3. At each pitch

1. Phone on your spot, tap **Measure**, don't touch anything for 3 seconds.
2. Do what the app says ("Rear left: step 2"), then tap **Wedges placed**.
3. **Measure again.** Repeat until it says **Level**.
4. Caravans: the app walks you through its four steps: side to side while
   hitched, unhitch, jockey wheel, corner steadies.

Tap a wheel in the picture if you put something different under it than
suggested. Start a new pitch with ⋮ → *New pitch*.

## 4. Tell me how it went

After levelling at a pitch:

1. ⋮ → **Share session data** and send it to me (WhatsApp, e-mail, …). It
   contains your vehicle and wedge settings and the measurements, nothing
   else. The app itself never goes online.
2. Add a few words:
   - Did the result feel level? If you have a spirit level, what does it
     say?
   - How many rounds did it take? Was any advice wrong or confusing?
   - Your phone model, if it isn't a Pixel.

## Known limitations

- Only tested on a Google Pixel 10 Pro so far.
- A phone lying slightly **turned** on its spot can show a small side
  tilt that isn't there. That's why it should rest against a straight edge.
- Caravan dimensions from the size picker are estimates. Measuring
  improves the advice a little.
